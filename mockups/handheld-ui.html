Why it looks like this

    

      
Battery
    
        
  • Black is off on AMOLED, so every screen is drawn as light on black. No filled cards, no backgrounds, no gradients.
    • 
        
  • The pet's face is a dark windshield mask. Only the eyes and the sign glow, so the hero element costs little.
    • 
        
  • Status text uses sign amber, which has no blue subpixel. Blue OLED subpixels draw the most power and age fastest.
    • 
        
  • Dim mode swaps the pet to an outline with lit eyes and hides everything but the time and weather, cutting lit pixels to about a quarter.
    • 
        
  • The dimmed frame shifts a few pixels each minute so the always-on pet doesn't burn in.
    • 
        
  • Rows away from the center of the list are drawn dimmer. That reads as focus and lights fewer pixels.
    • 
      

      
Responsiveness
    
        
  • Everything is solid fills, bitmap text and arcs: things LVGL draws fast on an ESP32-S3 without alpha-blending the whole frame.
    • 
        
  • The pet moves in whole sprite pixels (5 screen px), so each animation step redraws only the 160 × 160 sprite box, not the screen.
    • 
        
  • The sign scrolls inside an 80 × 25 px window, the cheapest possible "the pet is alive" signal.
    • 
        
  • Screen changes are short slides (about 200 ms). If full-frame slides stutter on the QSPI panel, cut instead and let the pet do a hop.
    • 
        
  • In dim mode, animation drops to blinks only, about 2 frames per second.
    • 
      

      
Ease of use
    
        
  • Home answers "should I hurry?" without a swipe: the pet's mood, its sign, and one line naming the stop and minutes.
    • 
        
  • Rows are about 92 px tall, roughly 9 mm on this panel, a comfortable fingertip. The tab bar's touch zone is the whole top band, not just the pill.
    • 
        
  • Everything sits inside the circle. Rows narrow and shrink as they near the edge instead of getting cut off.
    • 
        
  • Each gesture does one thing per screen: up opens stops, down goes home, right goes back. Small chevrons hint at each.
    • 
        
  • Tapping the pet makes its sign say why it feels that way, so the mood is never a mystery.
    •
