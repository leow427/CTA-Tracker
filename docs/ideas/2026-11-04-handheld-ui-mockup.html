Why it looks like this

Same as the app

  • Colors come from the app's design kit: night navy, raised navy, mist for secondary text, headlight yellow for the active control, cream behind Trackie, amber only for Delayed.
  • Every route is a card in its line's color: a darker shade of the line as the fill, a lighter shade for all its text (the same LineColors.card and cardText math, ported). Buses share the bus blue.
  • Cards, tabs, chips, and Trackie's tile have stepped pixel corners, a rim, light from the top, and a hard edge under them, so they sit up like game tiles.
  • Minutes use the app's 5×7 pixel digits, like a departure board. Route names are heavy and rounded.
  • Trackie is the app's 32×32 sprite, with the same faces and floating extras for each mood.

On the ESP32

  • Pixel corners are three rectangles each, so LVGL fills them with no anti-aliasing. A card is one vertical gradient plus a darker rectangle for its edge.
  • Trackie is an indexed 32×32 image drawn at 4×. His bob moves him one art pixel twice a second, so only his tile redraws.
  • The pixel digits are a bitmap font at whole scales only (3×–7×), so they stay crisp.
  • SF Pro Rounded can't ship on a non-Apple device, so the mockup uses Nunito Black as the stand-in. Convert it with lv_font_conv at the four sizes used.
  • The map draws the L lines itself from data/train_shapes.json (15 KB, built into the firmware), in whole pixels at half resolution, doubled. Over BLE it needs only your location (already sent), the nearby stops' coordinates (about 160 bytes more in the nearby message), and train positions (about 10 bytes a train, every 60 s). No map images cross BLE.
  • Screen changes are short slides (about 200 ms). If full-frame slides stutter on the QSPI panel, cut instead and let Trackie hop.

Battery and use

  • Awake screens are navy like the app, not black. Navy is dim, but the whole panel glows, so check the power meter against an all-black frame.
  • Dim always-on mode drops to black with Trackie alone, and shifts a few pixels every 10 s so he doesn't burn in.
  • Home answers "should I hurry?" without a swipe: Trackie's face, his speech bubble, and one card for the most urgent stop.
  • Cards away from the list's focus shrink and fade, and narrow to stay inside the circle.
  • Tapping Trackie makes his bubble say why he feels that way.

Questions for your critique

  1. Navy instead of black on the awake screens: worth the battery cost? Compare the power numbers.
  2. Trackie on the cream tile at Home (like the app), or straight on navy?
  3. His bubble says a mood word ("Go now!") and switches to the reason when tapped. Right split?
  4. Train rows are one card per station, colored by its soonest line. One card per line and direction instead, like the app?
  5. The map: a handheld screen for the alpha, or a stretch item after the core path works? It's not in the spec yet.
  6. Spec 8.1 still says "the pet centered on black" and rows with a line-color dot. Update it to this look if you like it.
