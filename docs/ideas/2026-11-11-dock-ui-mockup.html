Dock screen

The dock's 4.3" Waveshare panel, portrait, 480×800, drawn pixel for pixel with only what the handheld's gfx2d canvas can draw, so each function ports to C++ the way the handheld mockup became firmware/handheld/mock/src/ui.cpp. The faceplate to the right stands in for the three LED rows; it's hardware, not part of the screen.

Tap the map button in the top bar, or swipe left on the stops, for the map; drag to pan it, tap the pin button to recenter, and swipe right from its left edge (or tap the button again) to come back. Tap Trackie to ask why. Tap an empty section, or long-press any section, to pick a stop. In the picker, tap to choose, scroll the stop list, and tap the chevron to go back.

Porting notes

Drawing calls

  • All drawing goes through one small Canvas object whose methods match gfx2d.h: clear, fillRect, fillPixelShape, plate, chip, text, textOutlined, pixelText, ink, sprite, fillCircle, arrowCircle, line. Nothing else touches the HTML canvas.
  • Every coordinate is a whole pixel, and every size is a named constant at the top of the script (STRIP_H, SEC_H, CARD, TILE, PK).
  • Colors are 0xRRGGBB numbers. Card colors use the same th() math as ui.cpp: line shades for trains, white with gray text and a light-blue outline for buses, and the deeper Blue.
  • Trackie, his faces, extras, umbrella and scarf are the grids from art/, drawn at 2× (64 px, spec 7.3). On the device they come from pet::kPet through cv.sprite.
  • Taps are plain rectangles collected while drawing (hit()), the way ui.cpp keeps Rects.

Fonts used across every state

Nunito, as weight/size. A highlighted size isn't in the handheld's atlases (font_build.html) yet, so it would need adding.

Open questions to critique

  • The picker and pairing screens borrow the handheld's list and pairing styles; the spec only gives their steps.
  • The LED rows follow the CAD render: one row of 7 to the right of each section, LED 7 (the picked stop) on the right.
  • The map frames the three approach zones (each pick and the 6 stops before it, the same stops as its LED row), so a vehicle sits where its LED says. Lines nobody picked are dimmed; the 22's line would come from its cached getpatterns points. seg (block lines) and dot are the two new drawing calls.
  • Each tile shows the destination under the minutes. On most train platforms every train goes to the same terminal, so it repeats the direction; it matters for short turns and bus branches.
