Diorama

Chicago as a warm-white architectural model. The L lines are the only color in the city.

Pixel art appears in three places: the pet, the vehicles, and the live signal. Everything else is crisp and modern.

Floating controls and the sheet are glass. Cards are plain white with one line-color badge, and the minutes are set in heavy rounded type.

Drag to orbit (right-click or Ctrl-drag tilts). This is a scratch mockup on open map data, not Mapbox: the real app adds sun shadows and night lighting.
